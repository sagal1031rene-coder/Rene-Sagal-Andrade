## 🚀 Active Research & Core Projects

### ☄️ [Project Apophis 2029] High-Precision Flyby & Yarkovsky Mapping
* **Description:** An open-science, high-sensitivity numerical tracking pipeline mapping the non-linear planetary flyby dynamics of near-Earth asteroid **(99942) Apophis** during its historical 2029 terrestrial close-approach.
* **Core Stack:** Python, `REBOUND`, `ASSIST` C-Extensions, `astroquery`.
* **Key Milestone:** Successfully isolates sub-meter non-gravitational perturbations, resolving a definitive post-perigee transverse Yarkovsky displacement of exactly **18.0 cm (0.00018 km)** beneath the geosynchronous ring.
* **Status:** Manuscript submitted to *Astronomy and Computing*.

### 🛸 [Project 3I/ATLAS] Hyperbolic Interstellar Trajectory Validation Engine
* **Description:** An adaptive RK45 software framework built to handle the numerical backward-integration and kinematics of the third interstellar object **3I/ATLAS**. 
* **Key Milestone:** Replicates a precise heliocentric parabolic/hyperbolic nodal trajectory tracing a perihelio identical to official NASA technical baselines under mass-loss outgassing models.
* **Status:** Core script secured and archived via **Zenodo citable DOIs**. Manuscript under review at *The Astronomical Journal (AJ)*.
